Features

Everything a field clinic needs

Designed around the real constraints of rural healthcare — no internet, limited devices, multiple staff roles.

📵

Fully Offline

Records are stored locally on-device using an encrypted SQLite database. No internet connection required — ever.

🔁

Local Network Sync

Devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot sync automatically via an embedded server. No cloud, no data plan.

🔐

AES-256 Encryption

All patient data is encrypted at rest with SQLCipher. PIN-based access ensures only authorised staff can log in.

👥

Role-Based Access

Nurses, doctors, pharmacists, and lab staff each see exactly what they need — nothing more, nothing less.

📄

CSV & PDF Export

Export patient records and session data as CSV for analysis or PDF for printed reports — directly from the app.

💻

Cross-Platform

Runs natively on Windows, macOS, and Android. One codebase, consistent experience across every device your team has.

How it works

Simple. Even without signal.

Set up takes minutes. The workflow follows your team's natural flow.

1

Nurse registers a patient

Captures demographics, chief complaint, and vitals. All validated and stored instantly on-device.

2

Doctor consults & prescribes

Reviews the intake record, documents diagnosis, and prescribes treatment — all from their own device.

3

Pharmacy & lab complete care

Pharmacist dispenses medication; lab records results. All devices sync automatically on the same network.
