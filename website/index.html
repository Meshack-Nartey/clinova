✦ Features

Everything a field clinic needs

Designed around real constraints — no internet, limited devices, multiple staff roles, and patients who can't wait.

📵

Fully Offline

Records are stored locally on-device using an encrypted SQLite database. No internet required — ever.

🔁

Local Network Sync

Devices sync automatically over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot via an embedded server. No cloud, no data plan.

🔐

AES-256 Encryption

All patient data is encrypted at rest with SQLCipher. PIN-based access ensures only authorised staff log in.

👥

Role-Based Access

Nurses, doctors, pharmacists, and lab staff see exactly what they need — nothing more, nothing less.

📄

CSV & PDF Export

Export patient records as CSV for analysis or PDF for printed reports — directly from the app.

💻

Cross-Platform

Runs natively on Windows, macOS, and Android. One app, consistent experience across your team's devices.

✦ How it works

Simple. Even without signal.

Set up takes minutes. The workflow follows your team's natural flow from intake to dispensing.

1

Nurse registers a patient

Captures demographics, chief complaint, and vitals. All validated and stored on-device instantly.

2

Doctor consults & prescribes

Reviews the intake record, documents diagnosis, and writes a prescription — on their own device.

3

Pharmacy & lab complete care

Pharmacist dispenses medication; lab records results. All devices sync over the local network.

✦ Security & Privacy

Patient data stays where it belongs

No cloud servers. No third-party analytics. Your patients' data lives only on your devices — encrypted and under your control.

🔒

AES-256 Encryption

Every record is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher. Data is unreadable without your PIN.

📵

Zero Cloud Dependency

No servers, no accounts, no third-party services. Your data never leaves your devices.

🔑

PIN Authentication

Each staff member has a hashed PIN. Access is role-gated and session-scoped.

📡

LAN-Only Sync

Sync happens over the local network only. No data is transmitted over the internet.

👥

Role-Gated Access

Staff only see screens and records relevant to their clinical role — nothing beyond.

🧾

Audit-Ready Export

Export full session records as CSV or PDF for reporting, audits, and handover.
